TU TigerPath

Support

TU TigerPath is an independent student project for Towson University's campus — not an official Towson University app.

Report a problem or ask a question: open a support request (a free GitHub account is needed), or contact the developer listed on the App Store page.

Emergencies

TigerPath is not an emergency service. Call 911, or Towson University Police at 410-704-4444.

Common questions

Directions don't start or my dot doesn't move. In the iPhone Settings app, open TU TigerPath → Location and choose "While Using the App" (or "Always" for directions with the phone locked), and turn on Precise Location.

My steps aren't filling in. Settings → TU TigerPath → turn on Motion & Fitness. Steps are read when you open the app.

Class reminders don't arrive. Settings → TU TigerPath → Notifications → Allow Notifications, then in TigerPath open Classes → Class reminders and turn them on.

A building, entrance or path is wrong. Please tell us where; campus data comes from OpenStreetMap and Towson University's public campus map and is refreshed monthly.

Delete my account. In the app: My account → Delete account. It deletes your login and all your data immediately.
