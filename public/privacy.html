TU TigerPath

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 12, 2026 · applies to the TigerPath iPhone app and the website obanex.github.io/tigerpath

TU TigerPath (“TigerPath”) is a campus walking guide made independently by a TU student. It is not an official university app. TigerPath has no ads, no tracking or analytics, and never sells or shares your data.

Without an account (guest)

Everything you set up as a guest — favorites, classes, settings — stays on your device. Nothing is sent to us.

With an account

Location

TigerPath uses your location on your device to show where you are, give walking directions and measure the distance you walk. Your location is not stored on our servers. With your permission, the iPhone app keeps receiving your location while your phone is locked only during a walk you started (turn-by-turn directions or Walk With Me), and stops when the walk ends.

Walk With Me: when you start a shared walk, your live position is sent to the people who have your walk link, for the length of that walk only. It is passed along in real time and not saved.

Motion & Fitness (iPhone app)

If you turn it on, TigerPath reads your daily step count and distance from your iPhone's step counter to fill in your Activity. Only daily totals are saved to your account, as above.

Notifications

Class reminders are scheduled on your device. No notification service sees your schedule.

Services we use

  • Supabase (supabase.com) stores accounts and the data in the table above, protected so each person can read only their own data.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website and its map files.
  • Open-Meteo provides the weather. TigerPath asks for the weather at the campus location, not yours.

The map, walking paths and campus data come from OpenStreetMap and TU's public campus map data and are stored inside the app, so looking at the map doesn't share where you are.

Your choices

  • Download your data: My account → Download my data.
  • Delete your account: My account → Delete account. This deletes your login and everything stored with it, right away.
  • Location, motion and notifications can be turned off any time in your iPhone's Settings app.

Children

TigerPath is meant for college students and visitors and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Questions or requests: TigerPath support.
